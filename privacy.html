Bloom — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 June 2026

Bloom (“we”, “us”, the “app”) is a personal plant-care companion. We built Bloom to keep your data on your device. This policy explains what limited information the app handles and why.

Information stored on your device

Your plants, photos, care logs, health records, reminders, and preferences are stored locally on your device using Apple’s SwiftData. This information is not uploaded to any server we operate and is removed when you delete the app.

Plant identification & health checks

When you ask Bloom to identify a plant or run a health check, the photo you choose is sent to Anthropic’s Claude API solely to generate a result that is returned to you. Photos sent for this purpose are not used to train models and are not retained by Bloom. See Anthropic’s privacy practices at anthropic.com/legal/privacy.

Location & weather

If you enable weather-based care tips, Bloom performs a one-time lookup of your approximate location on your device to fetch the local forecast. Your location is used only to retrieve weather data and is not stored or shared by us.

Notifications

Care and reminder notifications are scheduled locally on your device. They are not sent from, or routed through, a server we control.

Subscriptions

Bloom Premium is handled by Apple’s App Store and our payments provider, RevenueCat, to validate and restore your subscription. We do not receive your full payment details. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy.

Diagnostics

Bloom uses Firebase Crashlytics to collect anonymous crash reports that help us fix bugs. These reports contain technical diagnostic data and do not include your plant photos or care data.

Children

Bloom is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

Because your content lives on your device, you can delete any plant, photo, or log at any time, or remove all of it by deleting the app. You can disable weather location and notifications in your device’s Settings.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
